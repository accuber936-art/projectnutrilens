## The Problem

Tracking what you eat is tedious. Searching food databases, guessing portion sizes and typing in every meal makes most people give up within a week. TODO: add one real statistic or a user quote, with its source.

## Our Solution

NutriLens lets you take a photo of a meal and see its calories, protein, carbs and fat straight away, then logs it for you so you can see your daily intake without typing anything.

## Key Features

- Photo-based food recognition
- Instant calorie and macro estimate
- Automatic daily meal log
- TODO: add or remove features to match your concept

## How It Works (User Flow)

1. Open the app and point the camera at a meal.
2. NutriLens identifies the food and shows the nutrition breakdown.
3. The user confirms or edits the result.
4. The meal is added to the daily log and progress view.
